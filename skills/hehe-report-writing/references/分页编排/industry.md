# Industry Mode

Use for 行研模块脚本.

## Inputs

Preferred inputs:
- project control file;
- 行研状态卡;
- industry analysis drafts;
- market sizing notes;
- policy or macro notes;
- industry chain map;
- competitive landscape;
- competitor comparison;
- chart list or chart library;
- source verification notes.

## Page Types

| Page type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 行业定义页 | Define scope and avoid wrong comparisons |
| 市场边界页 | Show core, substitute, and adjacent markets |
| 规模/增长页 | Explain market space and growth logic |
| 政策/宏观页 | Provide context, not direct purchase proof |
| 产业链页 | Show value chain and key players |
| 竞争格局页 | Compare player types and positions |
| 竞品对标页 | Compare product, price, channel, claims, reviews |
| 趋势与风险页 | Explain what may change and what can fail |
| 行研小结页 | Hand off to user/company/integrated report |

## Evidence Rules

- Market numbers require source, date, geography, and口径.
- Policy evidence must include issuer and date.
- Public comments are qualitative evidence, not statistical proof.
- Competitor product pages can support product claims, not market share.
- If two sources conflict, keep both and mark `【待核实】`.

## Required Output Emphasis

Include:
- scope definition;
- key assumptions;
- evidence strength;
- chart candidates;
- which conclusions can enter PPT;
- which claims need more data.

## Common P0 Blockers

- Market口径 not confirmed;
- no credible source for key market number;
- competitor set not confirmed;
- confusing product competitor with company research;
- using broad smart home trends without returning to the project task.
